# Code of Conduct

CronBox follows a simple contributor standard: be direct, respectful, and focused on the work.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give constructive feedback with concrete examples.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions.
- Any conduct that would make collaboration unsafe or hostile.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block participants when behavior harms the project or its contributors.

If you need to report conduct privately, contact the maintainer through GitHub and ask for a private channel.
